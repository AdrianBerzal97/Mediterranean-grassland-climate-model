# Mediterranean Grassland Productivity under Climate Change

Ecohydrological modelling of Mediterranean grassland biomass production under historical and future climate conditions in Central Spain.

## Overview

This project investigates how climate change may affect Mediterranean grassland productivity across contrasting hydroclimatic environments.

The workflow combines field observations, meteorological data, soil properties and vegetation parameters with the SIMPAST ecohydrological model to simulate annual above-ground biomass production.

The model was calibrated using historical productivity observations and independently validated before being applied to future CMIP6 climate scenarios.

## Study area

Three Mediterranean grassland systems located along a climatic gradient in Central Spain were analysed:

- Mountain grasslands — Piñuecar
- Foothill grasslands — Colmenar Viejo
- Semi-arid flatlands — Tielmes

## Modelling approach

SIMPAST simulates above-ground grassland biomass at a daily time step by integrating:

- precipitation
- temperature
- solar radiation
- evapotranspiration
- soil water availability
- radiation use efficiency (RUE)
- water use efficiency (WUE)

Model calibration was performed using biomass observations from 2016–2023.

Independent observations from 2024–2025 were used for model validation.

## Climate change analysis

Future grassland productivity was simulated using six CMIP6 global climate models under four Shared Socioeconomic Pathways:

- SSP1-2.6
- SSP2-4.5
- SSP3-7.0
- SSP5-8.5

Climate projections cover the period 2021–2100.
